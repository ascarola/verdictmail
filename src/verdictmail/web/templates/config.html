Graymail is unsolicited bulk/commercial mail that isn't a security threat but isn't wanted either — promotional/marketing blasts, cold sales outreach, mass newsletters, and notification digests. This is a separate axis from the threat Aggressiveness above; whitelisted senders are always exempt.
